# Market Filter Analysis

## Scope
- Source: `reports/signal_performance_YYYY-MM.md` generated by `src.evaluate --report`.
- Entry price basis: next trading day open.
- Aggregates exclude incomplete 20-trading-day evaluations.
- Market sentiment definition: same universe as `src.scan.py` (TOPIX Small 1 / Small 2 / Mid400), ratio of stocks with close > MA75.
- Buckets: bearish `<0.40`, neutral `0.40-0.50`, bullish `>=0.50`.

## Key Findings
1. Existing scan logic already suppresses bearish (`<0.40`) ENTRY; no completed ENTRY signals appear in bearish buckets.
2. 2026-01 ENTRY worked in bullish conditions, but 2026-02 ENTRY lost money even though all ENTRY signals were also bullish.
3. Market filter threshold 0.40 is necessary but insufficient; a simple bearish/no-bearish split does not explain ENTRY degradation.
4. 2026-03 neutral ENTRY outperformed bullish ENTRY in this sample, so raising the threshold blindly may discard good setups.
5. Next tuning should inspect continuous market_sentiment, volatility/drawdown state, and MA25 depth together rather than changing only the 0.40 threshold.

## Recommended Next Action
- Do not immediately change `MARKET_BULLISH_THRESHOLD` from 0.40 based only on these reports.
- Add a second-dimensional analysis: market bucket x MA25 bucket for ENTRY/WATCH/REJECT.
- Prioritize identifying falling-knife conditions inside bullish regimes, because 2026-02 ENTRY losses occurred in bullish buckets.
- Keep `src.scan.py` unchanged until this interaction is quantified.
